Unit 5 – Centroid & Centre of Gravity

← Back to Mechanics Notes

1. Introduction

The centroid is the geometrical centre of a plane figure. It is the point at which the entire area of the figure may be considered to be concentrated.

The centre of gravity is the point through which the entire weight of a body acts.

Exam Point:
Centroid is related mainly to area and geometry, while centre of gravity is related to mass and weight.

2. Centroid

The centroid of a plane area is the point where the geometrical centre of the area lies.

For a symmetrical uniform body, the centroid lies on its axis of symmetry.

3. Centre of Gravity

The centre of gravity is the point at which the complete weight of a body is assumed to act.

For a uniform body having regular shape, the centre of gravity usually coincides with its geometrical centre.

4. Difference Between Centroid and Centre of Gravity

Centroid Centre of Gravity
Related to geometrical area. Related to weight of a body.
Used mainly for plane figures. Used for physical bodies.
Depends on shape and geometry. Depends on mass distribution and gravity.

5. Centroid Coordinates

For a plane area divided into several simple areas:

x̄ = Σ(Ax) / ΣA

ȳ = Σ(Ay) / ΣA

Where:

  • A = Area of each part
  • x = Distance of the centroid from Y-axis
  • y = Distance of the centroid from X-axis
  • x̄ = X-coordinate of total centroid
  • ȳ = Y-coordinate of total centroid

6. Centroid of Composite Areas

A complicated plane figure can be divided into simple geometrical figures such as:

  • Rectangle
  • Triangle
  • Circle
  • Semicircle
  • Quarter circle

The centroid of the complete figure can then be calculated using the area-moment method.

x̄ = ΣAx / ΣA

ȳ = ΣAy / ΣA

7. Centroid of Rectangle

For a rectangle of width b and height h, the centroid lies at its geometrical centre.

x̄ = b/2

ȳ = h/2

8. Centroid of Triangle

The centroid of a triangle lies at the intersection of its medians.

It is located at a distance of:

h/3 from the base

or

2h/3 from the opposite vertex
For a triangle, centroid divides each median in the ratio 2 : 1.

9. Centroid of Circle

The centroid of a circle lies at its centre.

x̄ = Centre of circle

ȳ = Centre of circle

10. Centroid of a Semicircle

For a semicircle of radius r, the centroid lies on its axis of symmetry.

Distance of centroid from diameter:

ȳ = 4r / 3π

The centroid lies along the axis of symmetry.

11. Steps to Find Centroid of Composite Area

  1. Divide the given figure into simple geometrical areas.
  2. Find the area of each part.
  3. Locate the centroid of each part.
  4. Measure x and y coordinates.
  5. Calculate Ax and Ay.
  6. Use the centroid equations.
  7. Obtain the final x̄ and ȳ.
x̄ = ΣAx / ΣA

ȳ = ΣAy / ΣA

12. Centre of Gravity of Composite Bodies

For a body made of several parts, the centre of gravity can be calculated using the weighted average of the positions of the individual parts.

x̄ = ΣWx / ΣW

ȳ = ΣWy / ΣW

Where W represents the weight of each part.

13. Symmetry and Centroid

If a plane figure has an axis of symmetry, its centroid must lie on that axis.

If a figure has two axes of symmetry, the centroid lies at their point of intersection.

Example:
The centroid of a rectangle lies at the intersection of its horizontal and vertical axes of symmetry.

14. Solved Numerical

Question:
Find the centroid of a rectangle of width 100 mm and height 200 mm.

Solution:

For a rectangle, centroid lies at its centre.

x̄ = b/2

x̄ = 100/2

x̄ = 50 mm
ȳ = h/2

ȳ = 200/2

ȳ = 100 mm

Answer:

Centroid = (50 mm, 100 mm)

16. MCQs

Q1. The centroid represents the geometrical centre of:
A) Volume only
B) Plane area
C) Temperature
D) Pressure
Answer: B) Plane area
Q2. The centroid of a rectangle lies at:
A) One corner
B) Midpoint of one side
C) Geometrical centre
D) Outside the rectangle
Answer: C) Geometrical centre
Q3. The centroid of a triangle lies at the intersection of:
A) Sides
B) Medians
C) Altitudes only
D) Diagonals
Answer: B) Medians
Q4. The centroid of a circle is located at:
A) Circumference
B) Centre
C) Outside circle
D) Diameter end
Answer: B) Centre
Q5. The formula for X-coordinate of composite centroid is:
A) ΣA/ΣAx
B) ΣAx/ΣA
C) Σx/ΣA
D) ΣA×x
Answer: B) ΣAx/ΣA
Q6. The centroid of a symmetrical body lies on:
A) Axis of symmetry
B) Boundary only
C) Outside the body
D) Random point
Answer: A) Axis of symmetry

17. Quick Revision

  • Centroid → Geometrical centre of a plane area.
  • Centre of gravity → Point through which weight acts.
  • Rectangle → Centroid at centre.
  • Triangle → Centroid at intersection of medians.
  • Circle → Centroid at centre.
  • Triangle centroid → h/3 from base.
  • Semicircle → 4r/3π from diameter.
  • x̄ = ΣAx/ΣA
  • ȳ = ΣAy/ΣA
  • Symmetry helps locate centroid easily.
