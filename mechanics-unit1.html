Engineering Mechanics - Unit 1

1. Fundamental Units

Fundamental units are basic units which are independent of other units.

Examples: metre (m), kilogram (kg), second (s).

2. Derived Units

Derived units are obtained by combining fundamental units.

Example: Force = mass × acceleration

F = ma

3. Dimensions

Dimensions represent a physical quantity in terms of fundamental quantities.

Dimension of force:

[F] = [M L T⁻²]

4. Principle of Homogeneity

According to the principle of homogeneity, the dimensions of every term in a physical equation must be the same.

5. Dimensional Analysis

Dimensional analysis is used to check the correctness of physical equations and to derive relationships between physical quantities.
